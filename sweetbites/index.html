About Us

Small batch, big heart.

Sweet Bites by LJ started as a home kitchen project and grew into a small bakery serving Apalit and nearby areas. Every cookie, banana bread, and graham puff is baked fresh — no mass production, just careful small batches.

From classic chocolate chip to matcha lava and campfire s'mores, every flavor is made to be shared, gifted, or enjoyed solo — whatever the day calls for.
