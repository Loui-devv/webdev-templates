About Us

A family kitchen turned neighborhood favorite.

Lola Rosa's Kitchen began as a small carinderia run out of a family home. Word spread about the sisig and the kare-kare, and six years later we're now a full sit-down restaurant — still cooking from the same handwritten recipes.

Every dish is made fresh daily, no reheated batches. We source our produce from the local Pampanga market every morning.
