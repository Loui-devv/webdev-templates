About Us

Ten years of styling in Malolos.

Hiraya started as a small two-chair salon in 2016. Today we're four stylists strong, but the promise hasn't changed — no rushing, no overbooking, and every client gets to decide the look they want, not the clock.

We use gentle, damage-conscious products, and each stylist has their own specialty — from a classic bob to full balayage.
